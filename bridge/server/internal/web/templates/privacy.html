Privacy notice

How your data is used when you link Navidrome to your Discord status.

Who is responsible

This service is run by the administrator of this Navidrome server. {{if .Contact}}Contact: {{.Contact}}.{{else}}Please contact them directly with any questions.{{end}}

What is stored, why and for how long

DataPurposeKept
Your Navidrome username, your Discord user ID and name, the date you linked and last used it, and an encrypted Discord access token To set your Discord status Until you unlink{{if .LinkRetentionDays}}, or {{.LinkRetentionDays}} days after it was last used{{end}}
A session cookie while you use this page To keep you signed in and protect the forms (strictly necessary; no tracking) At most 12 hours, in memory only
Server logs: time, IP address and Navidrome username of sign-ins and link changes Security and fixing problems {{.LogRetentionDays}} days
What you are playing: title, artist, album, timing and a cover link To update your status Only in memory while it is shown; not stored

Your Navidrome password is checked by Navidrome when you sign in here and is never stored.

Who receives it

  • Discord (Discord Inc., USA) receives your status: track, artist, album and cover. Who can see it depends on your Discord privacy settings. See Discord's privacy policy.
  • To find cover art, the server may look up the artist and album name at the Cover Art Archive and ListenBrainz (MetaBrainz Foundation, USA), Deezer (France) and Apple iTunes (USA). These lookups don't contain your name or account.
  • If the administrator enabled it, covers that can't be found elsewhere are uploaded to uguu.se, a public file host that deletes files after about 3 hours. Anyone with the link can see the image during that time.

None of this happens before you link your account. No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Your choices and rights

  • Linking is your choice; the legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by unlinking on the start page, or in Discord under User Settings, Authorized Apps.
  • Unlinking deletes your stored data at once and revokes the access token at Discord. Log entries expire after {{.LogRetentionDays}} days.
  • The start page shows what is stored about you. For a copy, correction or any other request, contact the administrator.
